# Claude directory listing

## Name

Mosyn

## Tagline

Study sets that build themselves on your phone.

## Description

Hand Claude a list, a reading, or a call sheet and Mosyn builds the study set it implies, with a
verified picture for every entry, live in the Mosyn Android app. Claude can also write a custom
study game for the set. You approve the connection once, in the app on your phone.

## Example prompts

- Turn these notes into a Mosyn study set.
- Here is the call sheet. Help me learn everyone's name.
- Build a study game for my newest Mosyn set.
