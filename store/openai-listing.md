# OpenAI listing

## Name

Mosyn

## Short description

Turn material into study sets on your phone.

## Description

Hand ChatGPT a list, a reading, or a call sheet and Mosyn builds the study set it implies, with a
verified picture for every entry, live in the Mosyn Android app. ChatGPT can also write a custom
study game for the set. You approve the connection once, in the app on your phone.
